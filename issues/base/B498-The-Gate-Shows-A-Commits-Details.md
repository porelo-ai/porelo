# [B498](README.md#b498) — The Gate Shows A Commit's Details

**Status:** Idea.

**What must be true.** Reviewing a task's work at the gate, the person sees for each commit its message, author,
committer and trailers beside the diff, so a commit's message and its `Assisted-by:` trailer can be judged where the
work is approved rather than after it was forwarded.
