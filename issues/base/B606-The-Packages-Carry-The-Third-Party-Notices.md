**Why this size.** the generator's call in the packaging build, one entry in each package, `debian/copyright`, and a check in
`package-check`.

Filed 2026-11-10 on the operator's word of 12:12Z (one generator for all, priority Medium), after the
Planner's reading of its own packages (PL11) found the notices incomplete.

**What must be true.** The `.deb` and the `.rpm` of porelo carry the license notices of all third-party code compiled
into `porelo`, `porelod` and the hooks, with the full license texts, generated by the build and never written by hand:

- as `/usr/share/doc/porelo/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES`, named in `debian/copyright`;
- in the `.rpm` as `%license`, `/usr/share/licenses/porelo/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES`, so `rpm -qL` answers it.

## Found

Read on main f1c5204bf: `dist/dist-deb/src/deb/copyright/copyright` names only porelo (GPL-3.0-or-later) and
porelo-wire's classes (Apache-2.0); the `.rpm` carries `LICENSE` and `LICENSE.Apache-2.0`. Compiled in and named
nowhere (runtime scope of `apps/app` and `daemon`): jackson-core, -databind, -annotations and -datatype-jsr310 2.21
(jackson-core embeds FastDoubleParser), picocli 4.7.7, snakeyaml 2.7, bcprov-jdk18on 1.85.2, dbus-java-core and
-transport-native-unixsocket 5.2.0, aircompressor-v3 3.10, slf4j-api 2.0.17, jspecify 1.0.0, and the GraalVM
native-image runtime. Fedora has no `/usr/share/common-licenses`, so the `.rpm` must carry the texts itself.

## What Is Asked

- The generator is porelo-buildtools', owned by Agent Machines and built by Agent Planner for it; this issue waits for it
  to be published and builds nothing before.
- The packaging build calls it over exactly the jars `porelo` and `porelod` are compiled from, and the GraalVM runtime's
  own notices.
- `package-check` fails a package that lacks the file, or whose file names fewer jars than the native build took in.
- `Parity` keeps comparing the two packages; the file's place differs on purpose, as `LICENSE` does.
